# Function's legal pages

One file per document. Edit the text between the backticks and the
`updated` date, open a pull request, and the app and website pick it up.

- `guidelines.ts` – Community Guidelines (studentfunction.com/guidelines)
- `terms.ts` – Terms of Service (studentfunction.com/terms)
- `privacy.ts` – Privacy Policy (studentfunction.com/privacy)

How the text is written:

- `## A heading` on its own line starts a section.
- `- ` at the start of a line makes a bullet point.
- A blank line starts a new paragraph.
- `**words**` makes words bold.
- Email addresses (like help@studentfunction.com) become links by themselves.
- Don't use backticks (`) inside the text.
